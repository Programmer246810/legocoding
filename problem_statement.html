Problem Statement

What did we try to solve?

Invasive plants are spreading in our community, taking over areas where native plants grow and reducing food and shelter for wildlife. Many people don't know how to identify invasive plants, where to find them, or how to remove them safely. Even after they are removed, seeds hidden in the soil can start growing again. This makes it hard to stop invasive plants from spreading and protecting local biodiversity.
