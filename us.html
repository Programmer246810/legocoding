All ABOUT US

We are a FLL team made up 5 6th grade girls. Our names are Arshia, Avani, Claire, Saanvi, and Shriya. We have been participating in FLL since 4th grade, in which we made it not only to States, but the National competition in California. We did this with our original team, the UCRs. The UCRs stand for the Underwater Coder Robots. Our team chose this name, because FLL had to do with robots, we coded, and the theme that year was underwater. We were one of the many teams from our beloved elementary school, Hillside. Sadly, the following year, when we were in 5th grade, the 5th graders from the year before left and became Middle schoolers. Now, we were an all girl team! We called ourselves the Indiana Janes, because the was archeology. That year, we made it to States! This year, the theme is biodiversity, our team name is the Poison Ivies, this year is the LAST year of FLL, and we have all our fingers and toes crossed and hope we make it to States this year.
