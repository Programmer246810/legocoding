Spotted Lanturnfly

The spotted lanternfly (Lycorma delicatula) is a invasive species from Vietnam and China and poses major economic and ecological threats! It is established in at least 19 states in the United States, D.C., and there is noted presences in 15 - 21 states. It is also invasive to Windsor, Ontario, South Korea, Japan, and parts of Eastern Europe. It's considered invasive because it is an economic threat (vineyards, orchards, hardwoods, and ornamental plants are harmed by spotted lanturnflies), it's actions have an ecological impact (173 plant species are fed upon, weakening trees and promoting sooty growth), and they also spread rapidly.
