Why did we create this website?

We created this website to help volunteers get rid of invasive species.

How does this website work?

We send a robot into the wild to search for invasive plants and when it finds one, it will come back and tell us exactly where the invasive plant is located. Next, on the website, we post where the invasive plant is. Then, the volunteers (or anyone who wants to help out) can go to the invasive species and get rid of it. Wait, what if I don't know anything about getting rid of invasive plants, but still want to help the environment? Not to fear, the INFORMATION section at the bottom of this page will tell you how to get rid of the plant safely and will tell you what materials you need.
