CHALLENGES

What challenges did we face? What challenges does a FLL team normally face?

1. Our robot was not balanced and it was leaning slightly forward. We fixed this by testing various wheels and tried to make the robot level.

2. The axle in the gear box wasn't tight enough and caused the gears of our attachment to slip. We fixed this by taking out the original axle out and replaced it with a longer one. Then, we tightened the axle, so the gears in our gear box didn't slip again.

3. Even when we tightened the axle, the gears kept slipping and the attachment didn't move the way we wanted it to. To fix this, we tried using a different style gear box. It still didn't work! Even when we had super light attachments, the gears would still slip and the attachments still wouldn't work properly. To fix this, we replaced the medium sized motors with big motors and created a new base for the attachments to go on. It worked!
