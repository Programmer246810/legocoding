CHALLENGES

What challenges did we face? What challenges does an FLL team normally face?

1. Our robot was unbalanced and leaned slightly forward. We fixed this by testing different wheels and trying to level the robot.

2. The axle in the gearbox wasn't tight enough, which caused the gears in our attachment to slip. We fixed this by removing the original axle and replacing it with a longer one. Then, we tightened the axle so the gears in the gearbox wouldn't slip again.

3. Even after we tightened the axle, the gears kept slipping, and the attachment didn't move as we wanted it to. We tried using a different style of gearbox, but that still didn't work. Even with very light attachments, the gears kept slipping, and the attachments didn't work properly. We fixed this by replacing the medium-sized motors with larger motors and creating a new base for the attachments. It worked!
